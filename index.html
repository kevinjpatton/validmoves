Sessions

How each session works

Read the assigned sections before we meet, try the practice on the chapter page, and write down where you got stuck. We spend the session on those spots and on the discussion questions.

Bring to each session

  • The sections you read and the exercises you tried (starred ones have answers in the back of the book).
  • One exercise you got wrong or weren't sure about, with your attempt.
  • Your notes from the "Questions for our session" box on the chapter page.
